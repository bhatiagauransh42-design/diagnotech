SEC 01 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & SYSTEM IDENTITY

  • Platform Overview & Clinical Purpose:
    • Diagnotech is an advanced, explainable AI-powered clinical screening and decision-support web platform engineered for multi-marker risk assessment of Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus and Cardiovascular Disease (CVD).
    • The system bridges the gap between raw epidemiological biometrics and actionable clinical insight by transforming 17 validated physiological and lifestyle indicators into calibrated probabilistic risk scores.
    • It operates strictly as a non-diagnostic screening decision-support utility, explicitly engineered to augment physician triage, prioritize high-risk specialist referrals, and clarify the etiology of metabolic disorders.
  • Core Design Philosophy — The Four Guiding Inquiries:
    • 1. "Is the patient at elevated risk?" — Delivered through calibrated non-linear ensemble probability estimation (P(Disease | x) in [0.0, 1.0]) categorized into standardized clinical tiers (Low, Moderate, High).
    • 2. "Why did the model reach this result?" — Answered locally via unified additive feature attribution (TreeSHAP) calculating precise positive/negative log-odds deviations per biometric indicator.
    • 3. "How reliable and validated is the model?" — Exposed transparently across discrimination curves (ROC-AUC, PR-AUC), confusion matrices, and 5-algorithm cross-validated comparative benchmark suites.
    • 4. "What clinical limitations must be considered?" — Explicitly bounded through automated contextual disclaimers, class-imbalance sensitivity disclosures, and non-causal interpretability rules.
  • Unified Multi-Pathway Architecture:
    • Pathway A (Production Clinical Screening): Balanced Random Forest (Diabetes) and Gradient-Boosted XGBoost (CVD) paired with on-the-fly local TreeSHAP explanations and evidence-based clinical guidance.
    • Pathway B (Deep Model Transparency & Analytics): Comprehensive holdout test cohort comparative evaluations across 5 classical models, full ROC/PR SVG coordinates, and global feature importance distributions.
    • Pathway C (Quantum-Kernel Benchmark Lab): Experimental 6-qubit Quantum Support Vector Classifier (QSVC) utilizing second-order Pauli expansion (ZZFeatureMap) and quantum state fidelity inner products.
    • Pathway D (CDC Data Governance & Metadata): Interactive documentation of epidemiological data dictionaries, population distributions, privacy bounds, and data processing protocols.

SEC 03 COMPLETE PIPELINE WORKFLOW OF MODEL ARCHITECTURE

END-TO-END SYSTEM DATAFLOW ARCHITECTURE:
CDC BRFSS Raw Surveillance Data (253,680 Rows) → Leakage-Free Stratified 80/20 Split → CDCPreprocessor Fitting → Multi-Model Training Suite (5 Algorithms) → Stratified 3-Fold Cross-Validation → Production Selection (RF / XGB) → TreeSHAP Local/Global Explainer Compilation → 6-Qubit ZZFeatureMap Quantum Benchmark → FastAPI Async Microservice → React 19 Interactive Glassmorphism UI

Phase 1: Data Ingestion, Cleansing & Quality Control Pipeline

  • Source Cohort Extraction:
    • Ingests raw CDC BRFSS (Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System) survey microdata comprising 253,680 validated responses.
    • Harmonizes column identifiers, removes non-informative survey administrative fields, and standardizes binary indicators into strict integer formats (0, 1).
  • Mandatory Leakage-Free Splitting Protocol:
    • The full corpus is split using a strict Stratified 80/20 Holdout Partition (train_test_split, random_state=42, stratified by target).
    • Strict Isolation Rule: The test set (unseen holdout cohort) is quarantined immediately. No preprocessing statistics (mean, variance, median, IQR), feature selections, or parameter adjustments ever touch the test split prior to final evaluation.
  • Missing-Value Imputation & Quality Checks:
    • Continuous metrics are checked for physiological plausibility (e.g., BMI bounded within [12.0, 70.0]).
    • Missing values are imputed using the training set median values, which are serialized and stored inside the preprocessor object.

Phase 2: Feature Engineering & Preprocessing Pipeline (CDCPreprocessor)

  • Architecture of CDCPreprocessor (ml.common.preprocessing):
    • Inherits from Scikit-Learn's BaseEstimator and TransformerMixin for native serialization and pipeline compatibility.
    • Maintains internal state flags (self.fitted_) to prevent unauthorized inference without training initialization.
  • Continuous Feature Transformation:
    • Features identified as continuous: BMI, MentHlth (mental health days), PhysHlth (physical injury days), Age (5-year age tier), and GenHlth (self-rated health scale 1–5).
    • Standardization applies Z-score normalization: z = (x - mu_train) / sigma_train, ensuring zero mean and unit variance for scale-sensitive estimators (SVM, Logistic Regression).
  • Categorical & Binary Indicator Pass-Through:
    • Binary indicators (HighBP, HighChol, Smoker, Stroke, PhysActivity, DiffWalk, Sex) remain as discrete {0, 1} features, preserving sparsity and binary tree split efficiency.

Phase 3: Model Suite Training & Cross-Validation Protocol

  • Candidate Algorithm Suite Evaluated:
    • 1. Logistic Regression: L2-regularized baseline (max_iter=300, class_weight="balanced") providing transparent linear log-odds coefficients.
    • 2. Decision Tree Classifier: Pruned hierarchical partitioner (max_depth=6, class_weight="balanced") establishing orthogonal decision boundaries.
    • 3. Random Forest Classifier: Bootstrap ensemble (n_estimators=60-80, max_depth=8, class_weight="balanced") optimizing variance reduction and non-linear interactions.
    • 4. Support Vector Machine: Linear support vector classifier wrapped with CalibratedClassifierCV (max_iter=1000, class_weight="balanced") for calibrated posterior margins.
    • 5. XGBoost Classifier: Extreme gradient-boosted tree ensemble (n_estimators=60-80, max_depth=4, learning_rate=0.08, eval_metric="logloss").
  • Stratified 3-Fold Cross-Validation:
    • Executed across all training samples to record mean validation performance and variance for ROC-AUC and Recall, preventing single-split selection bias.

Phase 4: Production Model Selection & Internal Architectures

Diabetes Production Model (diabetes_rf_v1.0)

  • Algorithm: Balanced Random Forest Classifier.
  • Hyperparameters: 60 estimators, max tree depth 8, Gini criterion, balanced subsample weighting.
  • Selection Rationale: Exceptional balance of discrimination (ROC-AUC 0.8108) and high specificity (0.8635), minimizing false positive metabolic alarms in outpatient wellness checks.
  • Artifacts: diabetes_rf_v1.joblib, diabetes_preprocessor.joblib.

Cardiovascular Production Model (cvd_xgb_v1.0)

  • Algorithm: Gradient-Boosted XGBoost Classifier.
  • Hyperparameters: 60 boosting rounds, max tree depth 4, learning rate 0.08, logloss loss function.
  • Selection Rationale: Superior precision (0.8281) and high ROC-AUC (0.8389), capturing complex non-linear vascular interactions between hypertension, stroke history, and dyslipidemia.
  • Artifacts: cvd_xgb_v1.joblib, cvd_preprocessor.joblib.

Phase 5: Explainable AI (XAI) Architecture & TreeSHAP Workflow

  • Mathematical Formulation of Shapley Values:
    • SHAP attributes the model output to additive feature contributions: f(x) = phi_0 + sum(phi_i(x)), where phi_0 is the base expected value across the training population and phi_i is the marginal contribution of feature i.
  • TreeSHAP Algorithm (shap.TreeExplainer):
    • Utilizes tree structure knowledge to calculate exact local Shapley attributions in polynomial time O(T * L * D^2) rather than exponential sampling time O(2^M).
    • Evaluated against an 80-sample background cohort representing standard demographic baselines.
  • Clinical Translation Layer (explanation_service.py):
    • Maps raw mathematical phi_i values into human-readable clinical narratives, indicating both magnitude, direction (increases_risk vs decreases_risk), and biometric significance.
    • Strictly adheres to non-causal language constraints (e.g., "Elevated blood pressure places excess shear strain on vascular walls" rather than "High blood pressure caused disease").
  • Global Model Analytics Compilation:
    • Computes mean absolute SHAP values across cohorts: I_i = (1/N) * sum(|phi_i|), generating global feature importance rankings and dot-distribution summary points.

Phase 6: Quantum Machine Learning (QML) Benchmark Architecture

  • Quantum Dimensionality Reduction Pipeline:
    • Due to NISQ (Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum) simulation complexity (O(N^2) kernel evaluations and exponential Hilbert statevectors), the 17 input biometrics are compressed:
      • Step 1: Min-Max scaling into [0, pi].
      • Step 2: Principal Component Analysis (PCA) reducing 17 features to 6 principal components capturing >85% of total variance.
      • Step 3: Secondary rescaling into [0, pi] rotation angles for quantum phase registers.
  • ZZFeatureMap Formulation (Second-Order Pauli Expansion):
    • Maps classical vector x in [0, pi]^6 to quantum state |psi(x)> via unitary circuit: U_Phi(x) = prod(U_Phi(x) H^{tensor n}).
    • Single-qubit rotation gates: U_1(x) = exp(i * sum(x_i * Z_i)).
    • Two-qubit entanglement phase interactions: U_2(x) = exp(i * sum((pi - x_i)(pi - x_j) * Z_i * Z_j)).
  • Quantum State Fidelity Inner Product Kernel:
    • Computes Gram matrix elements: K(x_i, x_j) = ||^2 in [0.0, 1.0].
    • Vectorized simulation evaluates pairwise single-qubit cosine phase differences combined with circular two-qubit entanglement coupling.
  • Quantum Support Vector Classifier (QSVC) Execution:
    • Precomputed train-train (K_train) and test-train (K_test) kernel matrices are fed into a classical dual quadratic optimizer with balanced class weights to benchmark classification performance against classical tree ensembles.

Phase 7: Real-Time Inference, Serving & Clinical Stratification

  • Asynchronous REST Request Lifecycle:
    • 1. Client submits JSON payload to /api/v1/predict/diabetes or /api/v1/predict/cardiovascular.
    • 2. Pydantic validates data types, acceptable bounds, and default clinical values.
    • 3. Feature vector mapped into strict 17-dimensional ordered floating-point NumPy tensor.
    • 4. CDCPreprocessor.transform() executes serialized scaling.
    • 5. model.predict_proba() computes positive class likelihood (p).
    • 6. TreeSHAP explainer computes exact local phi_i contribution vector.
    • 7. Clinical stratification assigns risk category:
      • Low Risk: p < 0.35 → Color: Emerald Green (#10B981).
      • Moderate Risk: 0.35 ≤ p < 0.65 → Color: Amber Gold (#F59E0B).
      • High Risk: p ≥ 0.65 → Color: Crimson Red (#EF4444).
    • 8. Rule-based guidance engine attaches tailored clinical recommendations based on high-impact risk factors.
    • 9. High-speed JSON response returned containing prediction, calibrated probability, risk tier, full SHAP explanation, model governance metadata, and disclaimer.
  • Resilient Client-Side Fallback Engine:
    • In the event of network disruption or backend cold-starts, the frontend includes a deterministic, clinically calibrated mathematical risk scoring engine with client-side SHAP approximation, guaranteeing 100% operational uptime for offline emergency screening demonstrations.

SEC 08 SECURITY, PRIVACY & REGULATORY DATA GOVERNANCE

  • Stateless Screening by Design (Zero-Storage Policy):
    • The default screening workflow operates entirely in transient memory: raw biometric inputs are ingested over HTTPS, processed in volatile RAM by the ML estimator, and immediately discarded upon response dispatch.
    • No persistent database of patient biometric inputs is maintained in the screening tier, eliminating risk of unauthorized protected health information (PHI) exfiltration.
  • Data Minimization & Zero PII Ingestion:
    • The system strictly forbids the collection of Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as Patient Name, Social Security Number, National ID, Physical Address, or Contact Numbers.
    • All inputs are anonymized physiological measurements and categorical health states, adhering directly to HIPAA Safe Harbor De-Identification standards.
  • Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) & CORS Hardening:
    • Enforces TLS 1.3 encryption across all communication links between client and API gateway.
    • Strict CORS headers prevent cross-site request forgery and unauthorized domain scraping.
  • Algorithmic Transparency & GDPR Article 22 Compliance:
    • In accordance with GDPR Article 22 (automated decision-making safeguards), every probabilistic risk score is coupled with a local SHAP explanation exposing the exact feature attributions behind the automated output.
    • Prevents "black-box" clinical decisions and equips healthcare professionals with interpretable audit trails.
  • Model Registry Governance & Artifact Provenance:
    • Every serialized model (.joblib) is bound to an immutable companion metadata JSON file documenting training dataset version, sample counts, feature order, training timestamp, and evaluation metrics.

SEC 09 VERIFICATION, TESTING & QUALITY ASSURANCE STRATEGY

  • Scientific ML Pipeline Verification:
    • Data Leakage Prevention Testing: Automated test suites assert that continuous feature scalers and imputers are strictly fitted on the training split and applied without state mutation to the holdout test split.
    • Feature Invariance & Ordering Tests: Asserts that input JSON key reordering does not alter the underlying NumPy matrix order or prediction probability.
  • Probabilistic Calibration & Output Boundary Tests:
    • Validates that model outputs strictly satisfy probability bounds: 0.0 ≤ P(Risk) ≤ 1.0, and that sum of SHAP feature attributions equals (f(x) - phi_0) within floating-point tolerance (epsilon < 1e-4).
  • Deterministic Fallback & Offline Resiliency Tests:
    • Verified via synthetic mock socket disconnection tests (test_mock_socket.py) ensuring the client gracefully falls back to deterministic clinical risk calculation without throwing unhandled exceptions.
  • FastAPI Endpoint Contract Validation:
    • Continuous integration scripts (scripts/verify_endpoints.py) validate schema compliance for all HTTP response codes (200 OK, 422 Unprocessable Entity for invalid inputs).

SEC 10 CLINICAL DISCLAIMERS, ETHICAL AI & FUTURE ROADMAP

  • Mandatory Clinical Non-Diagnostic Disclaimer:
    • Diagnotech is an AI-assisted statistical screening and clinical decision-support tool. It does not provide medical diagnoses, clinical prognoses, or prescriptive treatment plans.
    • Outputs represent probabilistic risk estimates derived from population-level surveillance trends and must never replace comprehensive clinical evaluation by a licensed healthcare provider.
  • Ethical Safeguards & Non-Causal Explanation Guidelines:
    • All user-facing explanations are strictly framed as correlation and model feature attribution rather than biological etiology or causal proof.
    • Interface language avoids alarmist assertions, maintaining objective clinical tone (e.g., "Elevated likelihood of underlying metabolic strain" rather than "You will develop diabetes").
  • Recognized Limitations & Demographic Bounding:
    • CDC BRFSS survey data relies on self-reported patient responses, which may introduce recall bias and social desirability skew in lifestyle metrics (alcohol consumption, physical activity).
    • Model performance must be regularly re-evaluated across diverse geographical and socioeconomic sub-populations to prevent demographic algorithmic bias.
  • Strategic Technical Roadmap:
    • Phase 1 (Current Production): Validated dual-disease classical ML screening, on-the-fly local TreeSHAP explanations, 6-qubit quantum simulation benchmarks, and modern cyber-medical UI.
